Neighbors helping neighbors

A little help goes a long way.

Grocery runs, pharmacy pickups, a ride to the doctor, help with a new phone, or just a friendly check-in. Ask for a hand, or offer one.

Ask for help or offer it

Tell us what you need or what you can do, and a neighbor will post it and help connect you. You can also ask on behalf of a neighbor who doesn't use the internet.

Helping safely

  • Meet at the doorHand off groceries and packages at the front door.
  • No cards or PINsNever hand over a bank card, PIN or password. Pay back with cash, check or a payment app after the errand.
  • Keep it simpleAgree on what's needed and the cost up front.
  • Tell someoneLet a family member know who's helping and when.
  • EmergenciesCall 911. This page isn't watched around the clock.
